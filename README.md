BusBuzzLK Sri Lanka
Introducing BusBuzzLK Sri Lanka, a revolutionary crowdsourced mobile application designed to enhance your travel experience! Built on the Android platform and powered by a robust Firebase backend, this app allows users to seamlessly track buses across Sri Lanka in real time.
With Bus Tracker Sri Lanka, users can easily add the buses they are traveling on, contributing to a community-driven network of information. As users share their bus journeys, others on the same route can instantly see the current location of the bus, helping them plan their trips better and reduce waiting times.
Key Features:

Real-Time Tracking: Monitor the live location of buses as they progress along their routes.
User Contributions: Add and update bus information, fostering a collaborative experience for all travelers.
Interactive Map: Navigate the bus routes effortlessly with an easy-to-use map interface.
Route Notifications: Get alerts for your specific routes to avoid delays and optimize your travel.
